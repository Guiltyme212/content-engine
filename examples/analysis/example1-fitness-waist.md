## Why it works

### 1. The hook is a promise + a deadline
"how to shrink your waist **this summer**:" — a desired outcome (shrink waist) bolted to urgency (this summer). The colon is doing real work: it's an open loop. It literally cannot be answered in one slide, so the only way to get the payoff is to swipe. That is the entire mechanism of a carousel.

### 2. Listicle structure = predictable, low-effort swiping
Every body slide is `N. [action] (parenthetical benefit)`. The viewer instantly learns the pattern on slide 2 and knows exactly what each next slide will give them. Predictable structure lowers the cost of swiping to near zero — that's how you get people to slide 7.

### 3. The parenthetical is the genius move
`No sugar after 6pm` alone is a boring tip. `No sugar after 6pm (lighter stomach)` sells the *reason* in three words. Every single tip pairs **action → micro-benefit**. This is what makes each slide feel worth reading and worth saving.

### 4. Lowercase, casual, "friend telling you" voice
"lighter stomach", "water weight drops" — no clinical hedging, no "consult a professional". It reads like a girl texting you her routine. Authenticity > authority on TikTok.

### 5. The app plug is buried, not front-loaded
The product mention (`I track mine in HealthMeter`) is on **slide 4 of 7** — the middle. By then the viewer trusts the list. It's framed as *"here's the tool I personally use,"* not an ad. This is the exact mid-carousel plug pattern in our playbook. It never says "download HealthMeter" — it just shows the app on a phone doing something useful.

### 6. Real, imperfect, first-person photos
Gym mirror selfies, a Stanley cup, messy bed, treadmill POV. Nothing is studio-shot. The realness IS the credibility — "a real person actually does this." AI-generated content has to earn this same feeling.

### 7. Saves > likes by design
People **save** how-to lists to "do later." 145.8K saves on 397K likes is a save-heavy ratio, and saves are the single strongest signal to the TikTok algorithm that content has lasting value. The list format is a save machine.

## The transferable formula

> **HOOK:** `how to [desired outcome] [timeframe]:`
> **BODY (×5-6):** `N. [specific action] ([3-word benefit])`
> **PLUG:** insert the product as *"the tool I use"* around tip #3-4, never the last slide, never a CTA.
> **VOICE:** lowercase, casual, first-person, no hedging.
> **IMAGES:** real / real-feeling POV and mirror shots, not polished stock.

## What to copy for our tenants (brand-agnostic)
- The `action (benefit)` slide skeleton works for ANY niche: finance (`Automate savings on payday (you never see it, never miss it)`), skincare, plants, food.
- Search-seeded hook: pick a real query people type and answer it.
- Mid-carousel plug where the app naturally solves tip #3-4.
